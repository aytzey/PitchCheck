# Additional parcel spin robustness — 2026-10-16

Additional spatial robustness; original primary verdicts, p values, checks and weights are unchanged.

Alexander-Bloch-style Haar random 3D rotations of nilearn fsaverage5 spheres.
Schaefer-400 centroids are normalized averages of sphere vertices excluding the
medial wall. The RH uses the reflected LH rotation; nearest rotated centroids
are assigned within each hemisphere. 1,000 spins, seed 0; p=(1+exceedances)/1001
in the registered expected direction. No multiplicity correction is attached
to these descriptive additional p values. Original Holm tests and verdicts hold.

Atlas: `c5ca6bb873d2e0d1`. Mean repeated-parcel fraction: 0.211003.
Nearest assignment is deliberately not bijective. Rotations are shared across
all contrasts; they do not shuffle labels independently or mix hemispheres.
Sphere hashes and result/source hashes are in [the JSON](2026-10-16-spins.json).
The NPZ and raw signed maps remain under `scratchpad/gpu/runs/`.

For WS08, the spin statistic is d_z of each pair’s ROI-weighted parcel contrast.
Weights are the fraction of ROI vertices in each intersecting parcel. Small
literature locations therefore have a parcel approximation; its observed d_z
is shown beside the original vertex-ROI d_z. This approximation can change an
effect sign and must not replace the registered ROI statistic or parcel rank.

```sh
python scripts/research/parcel_spin.py --selftest
python scripts/research/parcel_spin.py --prepare "$DATA/parcel-spins.npz"
```

Preparation uses the offline research venv with nilearn/nibabel; statistics
and cache loading need NumPy only. The installed nilearn already included the
sphere files; no network, paid API or new GPU work was used. Existing saved
WS08 float16 audit matrices were hash-checked and parcel means recomputed exactly
as in the prior CPU replay. HV prediction hashes were checked and its original
primary vertex correlation reproduced to 1e-12 before the extra spin analysis.
The helper and one pytest cover determinism, mirrored hemisphere geometry,
identity nearest mapping, planted smooth effects, tied ranks, cache compatibility
and unchanged primary suite results.


The offline entrypoints accept `--spins <npz>`; see the [ledger commands](README.md#additional-parcel-spin-robustness--2026-10-16).
Real S2 Llama replay with the option preserved all ten test dictionaries, primary
checks and positive-control verdict exactly. A synthetic HV on/off comparison
preserved its complete primary result and absent-template decision. NumPy average
tied ranks exactly matched SciPy on 14 arrays, including all real primary mask
sizes. No primary statistic definition or decision rule was changed.
