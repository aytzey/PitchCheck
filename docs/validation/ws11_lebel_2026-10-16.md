20-second trim. It cannot be silently treated as the pre-registered volume path.

The missing, **verified usable inputs** are:

  surfaces for UTS01–03's seven validation stories, UTS04–08's exorcism runs,
  and the specified separate repeated-story runs. A subject/task HDF5 filename
  alone does not establish the individual repeat arrays and run identities
  needed for the registered single-presentation group reliability ceiling.

  volume/surface coordinates, with affine/registration provenance. Public
  pycortex/FreeSurfer material is present, but this conversion was not validated
  as an immediately usable input. `mri_surf2surf` and `mri_vol2surf` are absent
  from this machine's PATH.
- Run-wise timing/preprocessing provenance sufficient to reconcile the HDF5
  extra trim with the +10-s onset, slice-time reference, detrend/z-score and
  edge-trim rules. Repeated-story availability and transcript matching would
  still need per-subject/per-run checks; neither is assumed from a directory name.
